MALLOC_CAP_EXEC includes the dual-use D/IRAM heaps as well as IRAM-only storage.
Therefore exec_free and internal|8bit heap_free overlap and MUST NOT be added.
CONFIG_ESP32_IRAM_AS_8BIT_ACCESSIBLE_MEMORY and SRAM1-as-IRAM are not enabled.

RTC address spaces are separate: 8192-byte RTC fast and 8192-byte RTC slow.
The current ELF loads 32 bytes into RTC fast and reserves 24 bytes in RTC slow.
Neither RTC region is enabled as ordinary heap; do not add RTC capacity to the
212072-byte heap budget or count the fast instruction/data aliases twice.

5. Networking and management costs absent from this physical image
The app_main fixture calls the local capacity runner. There is no esp_wifi component,
no Wi-Fi initialization, and no active lwIP/TCPIP stack or UDP sockets. liblwip was
built as a dependency but contributes no allocated text/data/BSS to the linked ELF.
core_control_receive, core_coap_* and wire core_edge_next/owner/activate entrypoints
are absent from the final ELF. Local core_edge_step, node and endpoint execution
are present. No CoAP server cache, Block1 workspace or outgoing exchange objects
are instantiated by the capacity fixture.
